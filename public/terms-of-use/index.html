Fax prepares a document and asks a telecommunications carrier to deliver it to a telephone fax number. The carrier dials a number on the public telephone network, and that network behaves the way it has for forty years: lines are engaged, machines are switched off, paper runs out, numbers are reassigned, and some destinations refuse calls from overseas.

So we deliver on a best-effort basis. We do not guarantee that any particular fax will arrive, that it will arrive within any particular time, or that it will be legible at the other end. The app reports the outcome the carrier gives us, as accurately as it is given, and you can resend a failed fax. Where a fax fails, that is the remedy.

Do not use Fax for anything time-critical or safety-critical, and never to reach emergency services. Emergency numbers are not fax destinations and cannot be dialled from this app.
